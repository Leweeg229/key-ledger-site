Key Ledger privacy policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Key Ledger is a key-custody app for shops. It is built so that your records never leave your device unless you choose to move them.

What we collect

Nothing. Key Ledger has no account, no server and no analytics, advertising or tracking code. We do not receive, store or sell any information about you, your business or the people and properties in your ledger.

What stays on your device

The key sets, owners, workers and checkout history you enter are saved only on your iPhone or iPad, in the app's own storage. iOS encrypts these files while the device is locked. Deleting the app deletes them.

Backups and imports

When you export a backup, the app creates a CSV file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. It goes only where you send it, for example Files on the device. Backup files are not encrypted by the app, so keep them somewhere you control. Importing or restoring reads only the file you pick.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the app schedules overdue reminders on the device itself. A reminder shows the set's tag and the holder's name, never an address. Nothing is sent to a server.

Calls and email

Tapping Call or Email opens your phone or mail app with the number or address filled in. Key Ledger does not see or record the call or message.

Children

Key Ledger is a business tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date before the change takes effect in the app.
