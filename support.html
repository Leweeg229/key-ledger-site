Key Ledger support

Help for Key Ledger on iPhone and iPad

Contact

Email keyledgerapp@gmail.com and include your device and the app version (Settings, About).

Getting started

Add key sets in Data & backup, one at a time or from a CSV file with tag, address and description columns. Then add the people who take keys in Workers. To try the app first, open the sample ledger in Data & backup.

Checking keys out and in

Tap Check out on a card, look up the worker by name or phone, choose a return time and confirm. To bring a set back, tap Return. For a set that stays out with no return date, choose Other date or time, then No return date.

Moving to a new phone

In Data & backup, tap Export backup and save the file to Files. On the new device, install Key Ledger, open Data & backup and tap Restore, then pick that file.

Reminders

Overdue reminders need notification permission. Check Settings in the app, which shows whether reminders are on and links to iPhone Settings if they are off.

Privacy

Your records stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.
